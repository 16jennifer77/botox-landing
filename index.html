01The treatment

Small doses. Precise placement.

BOTOX Cosmetic and Dysport are neuromodulators, prescription treatments injected in tiny, counted doses into specific facial muscles. Every line on your face is made by a muscle repeating itself. When the muscle under a line relaxes, the line softens, while the rest of your face keeps moving like it always has.

Treated areas run from the forehead, frown lines, and crow's feet to bunny lines, lip lines and corners, chin, and neck. Clients also come in about a gummy smile, jawline slimming, jaw tension, and the lip flip. The goal is a face that reads rested, never frozen, which is why placement gets more of our attention than product.
